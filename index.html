How to make a receipt or bill online

Both tools live on this page. Choose the tab that matches what you need to hand over:

  1. Use the Bill tab for an itemised invoice, or the Quick Receipt tab for a single payment. The Bill tab opens by default.
  2. Enter your business details once. They are remembered in your browser, and you can add a logo made from your name or your own image file.
  3. Add the customer, the items or the amount. Totals, tax and the amount in words update as you type.
  4. Preview the result, then download it as a PDF, save it as a PNG or send it on WhatsApp.

Bill maker for goods, services and tax invoices

The Bill tab creates a full invoice you can print on A4 or send as an image. It is built for shops, freelancers and service businesses that need a proper bill with items, tax and payment details.

  • Your currency, automatically: the currency and phone country code match your region, and you can change them at any time.
  • Goods or services: use quantity × rate for goods, or a single amount for each service line.
  • GST, VAT or sales tax: set your own tax name and rate. Indian users can also split GST into CGST and SGST.
  • Discounts, split payments and bank details: record a discount, part payments or your bank account so the customer knows how to pay.
  • Logo and branding: auto-generate initials from your business name or upload a logo that never leaves your device.
  • No login, no cost: nothing to install and nothing uploaded to a server.

Quick receipt maker for cash, rent and fees

The Quick Receipt tab produces a compact one-page payment slip for everyday transactions. It skips line items and tax, so a receipt takes about thirty seconds to create.

  • Rent receipts and deposit slips for tenants, landlords and paying guests.
  • Tuition, coaching and school fee receipts for teachers, tutors and institutes.
  • Freelance and consulting payments where a simple acknowledgement is enough.
  • Shop and retail cash sales that need a quick printed or WhatsApp receipt.
  • Payment modes: mark a receipt as Cash, UPI, Card or Bank Transfer.
  • Saved shop name: type it once and it is filled in automatically next time on the same device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a receipt or bill online for free?

Choose the Bill tab for an itemised invoice or the Quick Receipt tab for a simple payment slip, fill in your business and customer details, then preview the result and download it as a PDF or PNG, or share it on WhatsApp. No account is needed.

Which tab should I use: Bill or Quick Receipt?

The Bill tab creates an itemised bill or tax invoice with quantity, rate, discount, tax and payment details. The Quick Receipt tab creates a one-page cash receipt for rent, tuition fees, freelance work or any single payment.

Do I need an account or login?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything is created in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded to a server.

Does it use my local currency?

Yes. The bill maker detects your approximate region and picks the matching currency and phone country code. You can change the currency at any time, and the quick receipt also lets you pick a currency symbol.

Can I add GST, VAT or sales tax?

Yes. Switch on tax in the Bill tab, set the tax name and rate, and the tool adds it to the total. Indian users can also split GST into CGST and SGST.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. Your business details and uploaded logo are saved only in your own browser so you do not have to retype them. Your IP address is looked up once to suggest a currency and phone code, and nothing you type in a form is sent anywhere.

Can I send the bill or receipt on WhatsApp?

Yes. On supported phones the share button attaches the bill or receipt image directly. Otherwise the image is downloaded so you can attach it manually in WhatsApp.

Does the receipt maker work on a phone?

Yes. Both the bill maker and the quick receipt maker are designed for phones first, with large buttons, plain wording and portrait PDF or PNG output that is easy to send and print.
